# Dental Demo Boost

Build a premium, conversion-focused website demo for a fictional dental clinic called "Bright Smile Dental Studio."

DESIGN DIRECTION:
- Clean, calming, trustworthy aesthetic — soft whites, a warm off-white background, a deep teal or sage green accent, and a soft coral/blush secondary accent for CTAs
- Modern sans-serif typography (e.g. Inter or similar), generous whitespace, rounded soft-edged cards
- Should feel premium and reassuring — not clinical/cold, not cheap or templated
- Fully responsive, mobile-first

PAGES / SECTIONS (single-page or lightly multi-page is fine):
1. Hero — headline focused on comfortable, modern dental care; subheading on trust/expertise; primary CTA "Book an Appointment", secondary CTA "View Services"
2. Services — General Dentistry, Cosmetic Dentistry (Veneers, Whitening), Invisalign/Orthodontics, Emergency Care, Pediatric Dentistry — as interactive cards
3. Why Choose Us — trust signals: experienced dentists, modern technology, comfortable environment, flexible scheduling, insurance friendly
4. Meet the Dentist(s) — short bios with photos (placeholder), credentials
5. Patient Testimonials — 3-4 short reviews with star ratings
6. Before/After or Smile Gallery — placeholder image gallery
7. Booking/Contact section — appointment request form (name, phone, email, preferred date, reason for visit), clinic address, phone, hours, embedded map placeholder
8. Footer with social links, quick links, hours

Make it feel like a real, live dental practice website — polished, warm, and built to convert visitors into booked appointments. Use tasteful subtle animations on scroll (fade/slide reveals), hover states on cards and buttons.
